===============================================================================
FALSE POSITIVE MANAGEMENT
===============================================================================

Ensemble Voting Strategy:
- Requires multiple models to agree before flagging as HIGH risk
- Weighted voting based on individual model performance
- Risk ranking: High > Medium > Low > Normal

Healing Mechanism:
- Auto-blocked IPs have configurable healing window (default 30+ seconds)
- Automatic firewall rule removal after healing time
- Reduces impact of temporary false positives
- Maintains security posture while allowing recovery

Adaptive Thresholds:
- Risk thresholds calibrated per model based on validation data
- Dynamic adjustment based on traffic patterns
- Feedback loop for continuous improvement (partially implemented)

CONCLUSION

The CSCI480 Layered IDS/IPS system demonstrates strong detection capabilities
through a multi-model ensemble approach:

- Best overall accuracy: 92.04% (Gradient Boosted Trees)

- Real-time processing: 500-2000 flows/second

The system successfully combines supervised classification for known attack
types with unsupervised anomaly detection for zero-day threats, providing
comprehensive network security coverage suitable for academic demonstration
and further development.
